# Piece artwork

Only Chess.com Neo is used. Originals: https://images.chesscomfiles.com/chess-themes/pieces/neo/300/{piece}.png . Copyright belongs to Chess.com; no open artwork license is claimed.

Stone, ethereal, glass and diamond images are material edits of the matching Neo originals, generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Kings always use unchanged Neo originals. Artwork is separate from the GPL code/engine license.
